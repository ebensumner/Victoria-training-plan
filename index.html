Victoria's Training Plan
4-Week Return to Training · Vegan
Key Rules
LoadStart lighter than you think you need to. Increase weight only when all reps are complete and the last 2 feel like a 7/10 effort.
SorenessMild to moderate DOMS: train through it. Severe (difficulty moving normally): reduce intensity 20–30%. Never skip.
Hard daysReduce load 10–15% and focus on form. Do not skip.
Protein115–130g/day. Aim for 25–30g across 4 meals. Pea + rice blend or soy isolate protein powder.
PostureUCS (rounded shoulders) and APT (forward pelvis) correctives are in every warm-up and cool-down. Do not skip them.
